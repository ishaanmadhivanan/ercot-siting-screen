Build your own county score. Tick the factors you care about, set how much each one counts, and the counties are colored by the result. Nothing is scored until you tick something.

How the score is worked out

Each factor is put on a 0 to 100 scale across the 195 ERCOT counties: the best county gets 100 and the worst gets 0. Factors with a few very large values (MW, miles, counts) use a log scale so those counties don't flatten the rest. Where a county has nothing to measure, the factor is left blank and skipped.

A category's score is the weighted average of its ticked factors. The county score is the weighted average of the categories.
